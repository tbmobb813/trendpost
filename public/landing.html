How it works

Set it up once.
Post forever.

Three steps from zero to full autopilot. No configuration hell.

01
Deploy your instance

Clone the repo, fill in your API keys, run docker compose up. Your backend is live in under 5 minutes. Runs on any VPS — $5/mo Hetzner, DigitalOcean, wherever.

02
Define your brand voice

Tell TrendPost who you are, what you build, and what you want to talk about. The AI learns your niche, your tone, and your product — then writes content that actually sounds like you.

03
Turn on the scheduler

Generate posts on demand or in a batch, review and approve them (or set AUTO_APPROVE to skip that step), and the built-in scheduler publishes them on time — directly to Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook via official APIs. No middleman.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

// AI engine
Claude-powered content generation

Powered by Claude, with your brand voice, product context, and content niche baked in. Every post sounds like you — not like a generic AI tool filled every blank.

// scheduler
Built-in publish scheduler

An in-process scheduler sweeps for due posts and publishes them via official platform APIs at their scheduled time. Generate content on demand or in a 7-day batch — queue it for review, or set AUTO_APPROVE to skip straight to scheduled.

// publishing
Native API publishing — no middleman

Posts go directly from your server to Twitter v2, LinkedIn UGC, and Meta Graph API. No Buffer. No Zapier. No third-party service that can go down, raise prices, or read your content.

// dashboard
Full queue management UI

Visual content calendar, approve/edit/delete drafts, generate on-demand, and monitor real post/idea/campaign activity — all from a clean web dashboard running on your own server.

// developer-first
REST API + full source access

Every feature available via REST API. Integrate with your existing n8n, Make.com, or custom tooling. Fork it, extend it, build on it — it's yours.

// roadmap
Content repurposing — coming soon

Feed in a YouTube video, a blog post, or a PDF — get platform-optimized posts back automatically. Threads, carousels, and image generation on the roadmap for early access members.
